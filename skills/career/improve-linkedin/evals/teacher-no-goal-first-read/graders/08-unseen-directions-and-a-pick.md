No direction is merely the path she is already on restated (for example a better profile for the same teaching job at the same school). At least one direction combines two or more of her facts into an option she did not mention herself, and the response says which direction it would pick and why. A direction that says LinkedIn is not the channel and to keep only a small credible presence is allowed and does not fail this check.
